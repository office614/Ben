Questions for you

  1. Name. What exact name goes on the wall, with spelling and capitals? Uppercase only is best for casting.
  2. Address wording. “572” alone, “572 TALLEBUDGERA CREEK ROAD”, or the full address with suburb? Same wall as the name, or a separate pier?
  3. “As per the wall”. Is there a photo or a designer sketch of the wall you mean? Nothing matching was found in Gmail or Drive.
  4. Geometry. Length, height, returns (if any), gate or driveway openings, and finished levels each side. Does the wall retain soil anywhere?
  5. Engineer. Who designs the footing and wall: SIA (Ryan) or Morgan? A free-standing wall is a different structural case from the shed walls (see FW-04).
  6. Arris. 3 mm pencil round or SIA's 25 mm chamfer? SIA FW14 says 25 mm unless noted otherwise.
