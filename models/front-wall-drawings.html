Questions still open

  1. Address wording. “572” only, or “572” with “TALLEBUDGERA CREEK ROAD” as a second line? On which wall?
  2. Wall sizes. ARDEN wall length, address wall length, height, returns, and the distance from the boundary.
  3. “Gooseneck”. Two possible meanings. (a) A cranked gate track: a rigid gate leaf can't follow one, so I've drawn a straight track behind the wall line. (b) A gooseneck keypad/intercom pedestal: shown as optional. Which did you mean?
  4. Gate. Clear opening, height, material and weight. Do you want a keypad, intercom or letterbox?
  5. “Modelled as one”. One combined model of all the front walls (built here), or the walls poured as one continuous monolithic wall? A continuous wall needs agreed control-joint positions, because SIA wants no construction joints in an off-form face.
  6. Option 1 lighting. Light grazing up the lettered panel from a groove at its base (drawn), or a downlight in the panel's underside washing the wall below?
  7. Engineer. SIA (Ryan) or Morgan for the free-standing walls, footings, gate track footing and motor plinth?
  8. Arris. 3 mm pencil round or SIA's 25 mm chamfer?
