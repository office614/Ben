Arris detail

All exposed arrises: 3 mm pencil round, formed by a 3 mm radius on the formply edge or a 3 mm PVC arris insert. No fillet strip at external corners. Re-entrant angles at the seat step: as below.

Conflict SIA general note FW14 says “chamfer re-entrant angles and fillet all corners by 25 mm UNO”. A 3 mm pencil round departs from that. It is permitted only if the architectural documents say so (the “UNO”). Get written acceptance from SDM/SIA before tender close. A 3 mm arris is also more prone to grout loss and chipping at stripping, so allow for sealed sheet edges and late stripping.
