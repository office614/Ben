Four steps, one pass

Paste or drop a contract and every check runs in order — no clicking through wizards.

STEP 01

Clause-by-clause risk

Each clause weaker than or in conflict with EMC's terms is flagged and mapped to the exact clause number, with precisely what to strike or add — plus EMC protections that are missing and should be added.

STEP 02

Counterparty look-up

The AI review runs the search for you — ABN Lookup, ASIC and adverse-news — and reports entity status, registration and any court / insolvency / payment-dispute findings, with sources. (Offline scan gives you the registers to check by hand.) Public-record and news only — not a credit report.

STEP 03

Risk profile

A structured profile with an overall low / medium / high rating and reasoning, plus suggested fields to track next time.

STEP 04

Recommendations & response

Two ready-to-use documents: a recommendations memo to the Builder — each requested amendment with its legal reason (SoP Act / ACL / contract law) — and a direct response to the contract administrator. Open into Gmail, copy, print or export.

Two ways to review

Instant local triage, or a full AI read of the whole document when the stakes are higher.

Recommended

AI review

  • Reads the whole contract with Claude and reasons over it clause-by-clause — catches novel wording and nuance.
  • Uses your own Anthropic API key (stored in your browser); pick Opus 5, Sonnet 5 or Haiku 4.5. Roughly 10–30¢ a review.
  • Works on this hosted site and when you install the app to your phone.
Always available

Offline scan

  • An instant, fully-local pass over ~21 detection rules — pay-when-paid, time-bars, latent conditions, termination for convenience and more.
  • Free and private — nothing leaves your browser. Great for a fast first triage.
  • Runs offline once the app is installed.
