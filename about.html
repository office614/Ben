About us

A Canberra cleaning business built on turning up, doing the job properly and making it easy for our clients.

Our story

Local cleaners for the nation's capital

National Capital Cleaning looks after offices, commercial buildings, shopfronts and glass across Canberra and the surrounding region.

We started with a simple idea: businesses shouldn't have to chase their cleaners. Every client gets a written cleaning plan, a regular crew who knows the site, and a single person to call when something needs to change.

Whether it is a small office cleaned three nights a week or a multi-level building with external glass, we bring the same checklist-driven approach and attention to detail.
